# POTIFY Public Treasury Record marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Community members and independent reviewers. **Offer.** Help any reader verify a published treasury statement against a specific on-chain receipt.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish the format with a pending example and explain how to follow one verified receipt when available. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Review one treasury claim against its source. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 3 independently reproducible checks. |

### Sales test

Budget zero for acquisition. Track the time to independently verify a statement. If readers later buy merch, count only a separately attributable completed order. Support trust in the ecosystem; trace any later shop referral separately. No direct treasury sales target.

### Measurement and decision

Use `treasury-record-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** This is a new reporting format. Custody, current balances, vesting and signer control have not been audited here. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
